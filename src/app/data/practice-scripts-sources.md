## Notes on Script Length

All scripts are written to be delivered in approximately 60–120 seconds at their target WPM range:

- **Beginner** (110–130 WPM): ~120–160 words → ~60–85 seconds
- **Intermediate** (120–140 WPM): ~150–200 words → ~70–95 seconds
- **Advanced** (130–155 WPM): ~175–230 words → ~75–100 seconds

Scripts were shortened or paraphrased from source material to fit the target duration. For public domain speeches (Roosevelt, Kennedy), original text was used directly where length permitted, with additional framing sentences added.
